# Project Reports

This folder contains written reports documenting the findings and business insights from the Cenovus Supply Chain & Operations Analytics project.

## Planned Reports

1. `data_quality_report.md` — Documents data quality assessment findings and cleaning decisions.
2. `production_operations_analysis.md` — Presents production performance trends and operational findings.
3. `transportation_logistics_analysis.md` — Examines transportation efficiency, delivery performance, and logistics bottlenecks.
4. `business_insights.md` — Summarizes key findings, recommendations, and potential business improvements.

Reports will be based on actual analytical results. No findings or business conclusions will be assumed before the data has been analyzed.
