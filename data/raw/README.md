# Raw Data

This folder stores the original datasets collected for the Cenovus Supply Chain & Operations Analytics project.

The raw data may include:

- Oil and gas production records.
- Production facility and operational data.
- Transportation and logistics records.
- Shipment and delivery information.
- Supply chain and operational performance data.

Original datasets will be preserved in their collected form before data cleaning and transformation.

**Note:** Only publicly accessible or clearly identified simulated datasets will be used. No confidential or proprietary Cenovus data is included.
