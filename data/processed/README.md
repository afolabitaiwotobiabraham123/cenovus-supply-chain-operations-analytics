# Processed Data

This folder stores datasets that have undergone data cleaning, transformation, validation, and preparation for analysis.

Processed datasets may include:

- Cleaned production records.
- Standardized transportation and logistics data.
- Validated supply chain records.
- Prepared operational performance datasets.
- Analysis-ready datasets for MySQL and Power BI.

All transformations will be documented to maintain data quality, consistency, and traceability.

**Note:** Processed datasets are derived from the original raw datasets stored in `data/raw/`.
