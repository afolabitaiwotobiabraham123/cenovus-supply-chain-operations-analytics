# Cenovus Supply Chain & Operations Analytics

**Project Status:** In Progress

## 1. Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing supply chain and operational activities within an oil and gas operating environment, using Cenovus Energy as the business context.

The objective is to apply data analytics to understand energy production performance, transportation activities, logistics efficiency, resource utilization, and operational coordination.

The project combines Supply Chain Analytics and Operations Analytics to investigate how production activities connect with transportation and logistics operations, identify inefficiencies, and support better operational planning.

This is an independent portfolio project using publicly available or appropriately simulated data. It is not an official Cenovus Energy project and does not use confidential company information.

## 2. Business Problem

Oil and gas operations depend on effective coordination between production activities, transportation networks, storage facilities, and delivery schedules.

Inefficient production planning, transportation delays, logistical bottlenecks, poor resource utilization, and production-to-transportation mismatches can affect operational performance and increase costs.

The business problem is:

**How can supply chain and operations analytics be used to identify production inefficiencies, evaluate logistics performance, monitor transportation activities, and improve operational coordination within an oil and gas operating environment?**

## 3. Project Objectives

- Analyze energy production and operational performance.
- Examine transportation and logistics activities.
- Evaluate production-to-transportation coordination.
- Identify operational bottlenecks and logistical delays.
- Analyze transportation efficiency and resource utilization.
- Examine production and transportation capacity utilization.
- Identify patterns associated with operational disruptions.
- Evaluate supply chain and logistics performance.
- Develop operational and supply chain KPIs.
- Identify opportunities for improved logistics and operational planning.
- Present evidence-based findings through an interactive Power BI dashboard.

## 4. Scope of Analysis

### A. Operations Analytics

**Energy Production Analysis**
- Production volume patterns.
- Daily and monthly production trends.
- Production variations.
- Production performance comparisons.
- Production consistency and disruptions.

**Operational Efficiency Analysis**
- Resource utilization.
- Capacity utilization.
- Operational throughput.
- Equipment and facility performance, where available.
- Operational delays and disruptions.
- Performance variations across operational periods.

### B. Supply Chain Analytics

**Transportation and Logistics Analysis**
- Transportation volumes.
- Movement of energy products.
- Transportation performance.
- Delivery and transportation delays.
- Logistics activity patterns.
- Transportation cost analysis, where data is available.

**Production-to-Supply Chain Coordination**
- Production versus transportation volumes.
- Supply and transportation mismatches.
- Production-to-delivery coordination.
- Potential accumulation of untransported production, where supported by the data.
- Identification of operational bottlenecks.

**Logistics Optimization**
- Identification of inefficient transportation patterns.
- Transportation capacity utilization.
- Logistics scheduling analysis.
- Identification of potential bottlenecks.
- Comparison of alternative operational scenarios, where sufficient data is available.
- Evidence-based recommendations for improving logistics efficiency.

## 5. Data Sources and Datasets

The project will use publicly available energy production, transportation, supply chain, and logistics datasets or appropriately simulated data.

Potential data sources will be identified and evaluated based on their relevance to:

- Oil and gas production.
- Energy transportation.
- Pipeline and logistics operations.
- Production and delivery volumes.
- Transportation performance.
- Operational capacity and utilization.
- Logistics costs and delivery timelines.

The selected datasets will be documented, assessed, and prepared before analysis.

Where public datasets do not contain sufficient information for specific operational questions, appropriately simulated data may be considered and clearly identified.

No Cenovus internal or confidential operational data will be assumed to be publicly available.

## 8. Project Workflow

1. Identify and acquire relevant energy operations and supply chain datasets.
2. Explore the datasets using Python and Jupyter Notebook.
3. Assess data quality.
4. Clean and prepare the datasets using Python and Pandas.
5. Store prepared datasets in MySQL.
6. Perform energy production and operational analysis using SQL.
7. Perform transportation and logistics analysis.
8. Calculate relevant operations and supply chain KPIs.
9. Investigate production-to-logistics coordination and operational efficiency.
10. Identify potential optimization opportunities supported by the data.
11. Develop an interactive Power BI dashboard.
12. Document findings, business insights, and recommendations.
